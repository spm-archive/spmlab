Electrophysiological Classification

Our work explores the acquisition, processing, and classification of electrophysiological signals such as EMG and EEG. By combining advanced sensing technologies with stretchable electronics, we enable next-generation systems for health monitoring, rehabilitation, and human-machine interaction.
